# Common questions

## Is the platform free?

Yes. Hook is free for both clients and providers during launch. You pay the provider directly for their work; we are not in that loop.

## Who built Hook?

Hook is built and operated by a small independent team. The team identity is not published publicly. WhatsApp is the only channel through which you reach us.

## How do I cancel?

Reply `CANCEL` to drop the current draft. To unlist as a provider, reply `LEAVE`.

## Who can read my messages?

In the private chat link: only you and the other participant. The chat is end-to-end encrypted; the server holds ciphertext only. On the WhatsApp channel itself: the bot and Meta (per the WhatsApp Business Platform rules).

## Is there a human support agent?

No. Hook is bot-operated. WhatsApp is the only support channel and you are talking to the bot now.

## Which languages does the bot understand?

The bot detects the language of your message and replies in the same language. English works best; many other languages are supported via the underlying AI model.

## I shared the wrong contact / wrong service

Send `NEW` to close the current request and start a fresh one, or `CANCEL` to abandon the current draft.

## How does ranking work?

Providers are ranked by a combined score of distance, recent activity, and past feedback. Hook does not accept payment for placement.
