# What Hook is

Hook is a WhatsApp-first marketplace that connects people who need a service ("clients") with nearby providers who offer it. The bot runs the entire funnel over WhatsApp; you never install an app.

## Two flows

- **Request a service (client flow).** Tell the bot what you need ("I need a plumber"), share your location, and the bot matches you to nearby providers.
- **Register as a provider (provider flow).** Tell the bot what you do ("I offer carpentry"), share your location, and you become listed. The bot pings you when a nearby client picks you.

## Two ways the bot connects you

- **Contact exchange.** The bot shares the provider's phone number with the client (and vice versa); the two of you take the conversation off-platform on WhatsApp.
- **Private chat link.** The bot opens an ephemeral, end-to-end encrypted chat between you and the provider. No phone numbers are exchanged.

## What Hook is not

Hook is not a hiring platform, not a payments platform, not a verification service. We do not vet providers and we do not process money. Quality is signalled by feedback, which feeds a trust score we use to rank providers.

## Support

WhatsApp is the only support channel. There is no email, no phone line, no human agent.
